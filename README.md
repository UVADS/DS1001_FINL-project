# DS1001_FINL-project

## Column Descriptions

Age: The age of the customer. It represents the customer's age at the time of data collection and can provide insights into age-based banking behaviors and preferences. 

Job: Refers to the occupation or job type of the customer, such as management, technician, student, unemployed, etc. It helps in understanding the employment profile of customers and its influence on banking activities. 

Marital: Indicates the marital status of the customer, such as married, single, divorced, or widowed. Marital status can impact financial decision-making and banking needs. 

Education: Describes the educational background of the customer, such as primary, secondary, tertiary, or unknown. It provides information on the level of education attained by customers and its potential correlation with banking behaviors. 

Race: Individuals self-identified race 

Default: Indicates whether the customer has defaulted on any previous loans or credit. It is a binary variable, with "yes" representing a customer who has defaulted and "no" representing a customer who has not. 

Balance: The balance or account balance of the customer. It represents the amount of money held in the customer's account at the time of data collection. 

Housing: Specifies whether the customer has a housing loan. It is a binary variable, with "yes" indicating a customer with a housing loan and "no" indicating a customer without one. 

Contact: Refers to the communication contact method used for the customer, such as cellular, telephone, or unknown. It provides insights into the preferred communication channels for reaching customers. 

Duration: The duration, in seconds, of the last contact made with the customer during the campaign. It provides information on the length of customer interactions. 

contactndays: The number of days that passed since the customer was last contacted in a previous campaign. If the customer was not contacted previously, the value is set to -1. 

Signed Up: It indicates whether the customer subscribed to a new credit card or not.  

 Gender: Individuals self-identified gender
